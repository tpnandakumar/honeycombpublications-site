About the book

This is not a book of easy answers. It is a collection of principles forged through reflection, observation, failure and continual growth. It explores purpose, discipline, resilience, character, leadership and the long work of becoming.

A father sets down the lessons no school can teach: how to hold discipline under pressure, build character when no one is watching, and turn error into experience and experience into wisdom.

The question is not whether you will make mistakes. It is whether your son must make the same ones.
