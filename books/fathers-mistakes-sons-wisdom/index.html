About the book

This is not a book of easy answers. It is a collection of principles forged through reflection, observation, failure and continual growth. Drawing strength from the Bhagavad Gita, Marcus Aurelius, Seneca and the Stoic tradition, it explores purpose, discipline, resilience, character, leadership and the long work of becoming.

A father sets down the lessons no school can teach: how to hold discipline under pressure, how to build character when no one is watching, how to turn error into experience and experience into wisdom.

Learnings become experience. Experience becomes wisdom. Wisdom becomes character. Character builds culture, and culture paves the path of destiny.

The question is not whether you will make mistakes. It is whether your son must make the same ones.
