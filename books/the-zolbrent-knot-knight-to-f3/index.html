About the book

Alex Winston sees the world too clearly and holds it too tightly. Gifted, watchful and quietly ruinous, he is a boy whose single talent is to know what is coming and whose greatest torment is that knowledge has never been enough.

Around him move a physicist father who will not soften a single true thing, a mother whose love is a kind of weather, and three children who will each teach him what he is for. Above them, a falcon watches. Beneath cold northern water, something older waits.

Each of the 36 chapters is a real chess move, so the novel itself becomes the game. But Alex does not yet know who is playing him, or what the final move will cost.

The board is already set. The knight has moved. And somewhere beyond the horizon, the answer is moving back.
