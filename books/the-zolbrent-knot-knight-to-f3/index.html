About the book

Alex Winston sees the world too clearly and holds it too tightly. Gifted, watchful and quietly ruinous, he is a boy whose single talent is to see exactly what is coming and never to be able to stop it.

Around him move a physicist father who will not soften a single true thing, a mother whose love is a kind of weather, and three children who will each teach him what he is for. Told as a chess game played move by move, Knight to F3 opens The Zolbrent Knot saga as a luminous, dread-laced coming-of-age story about fire, water, stillness and the terrible cost of a gift.

What makes it distinctive

Each of the 36 chapters is an actual chess move, so the book itself becomes the game. The story combines literary prose, mythic imagery, a recurring falcon, the Norwegian Gold Mane and twelve original watercolour-ink illustrations.
