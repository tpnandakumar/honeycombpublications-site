About the book

There is something invisible, and yet, to the chosen ones, entirely visible.

Some have felt it. Some know the one it watches. The clever, the careless, the quiet, those who still enough to feel the weight of its attention may one day turn, and find it watching.

It has waited a long time. It is patient. It misses nothing.

And somewhere, on a road that appears on no map, someone who does not yet know what they are for is about to find out.

Legends are not born. They are sculpted by nature, forged by choice.
