About the book

A cardiac implant rolled out across the NHS quietly becomes an instrument of the state. Behind the device sits an artificial intelligence chip that scores each patient on the cost they place against the value they return.

A young cardiology trainee begins to notice a pattern the numbers were built to hide.

Then the ledger opens again, and this time it contains a name she knows.
