Some people are valuable. Some waste resources. Where do you draw the line—and who holds the pen?

A voluntary NHS cardiac implant conceals a programme that scores each patient on cost against worth. When the ledger judges a person too expensive to keep, the device induces a fatal arrhythmia that reads as natural. A registrar begins to see the pattern the numbers were built to hide.

The price a society puts on a human life. Medicine turned against the patient. Trust, stewardship, and the machinery of the state.
