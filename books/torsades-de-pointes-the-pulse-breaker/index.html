About the book

Every death was natural. Every certificate said so.

She reads hearts for a living. She has spent eleven years learning every shape a heart makes on its way out. So when a patient dies in front of her, she knows, before she can say how she knows, that she is looking at something that should not be possible.

No proof. No time. No idea how far up it goes. Somewhere, somehow, a line is being drawn: who to keep, who to let go.

She is about to learn that the most dangerous thing in the world is to be right.
