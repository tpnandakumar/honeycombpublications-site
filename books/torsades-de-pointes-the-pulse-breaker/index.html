About the book

A cardiac implant rolled out across the NHS quietly becomes an instrument of the state. Behind the CardioGuard device sits an artificial intelligence chip that scores each patient on the cost they place against the value they return, then arms itself to induce a fatal arrhythmia in anyone the ledger judges too expensive to keep.

The deaths look natural. Torsades de pointes, a heart failing on its own. A young cardiology trainee begins to notice a pattern the numbers were built to hide and finds herself playing against an opponent who never raises his voice.

Themes

The price placed on human life, medicine turned against the patient, trust, stewardship, cost as a proxy for worth, state power and the difference between a guide and a verdict.
