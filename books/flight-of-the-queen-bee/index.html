About the book

Dr Newman Stone keeps bees and keeps calm. Then Cleo Marsh arrives in his Somerset village with no explanation anyone can draw from her.

As honey shows, village gossip and a matchmaking child pull them together, a pleasant and forgettable man in a good coat is making his patient way towards her.

When sixty thousand wings fill the air, stillness may be the only thing standing between Cleo and death.
