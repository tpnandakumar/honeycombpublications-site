Flight of the Queen Bee

He has never feared a swarm. She has every reason to fear the man following her.

A village romance with hidden danger

Dr Newman Stone keeps bees and keeps calm. Then Cleo Marsh arrives in his Somerset village with no explanation anyone can draw from her.

When sixty thousand wings fill the air, stillness may be the only thing standing between Cleo and death.

About the book

As honey shows, village gossip and a matchmaking child pull Newman and Cleo together, while a pleasant and forgettable man in a good coat makes his patient way towards her.
