About the book

Dr Newman Stone keeps bees and keeps calm. In thirty-eight years, his hands have never once shaken. Then Cleo Marsh arrives in his Somerset village with no explanation anyone can draw from her, having chosen the one place where a person might disappear without realising it is also the one place where nothing stays hidden.

As Newman and Cleo are drawn together through honey shows, cooking clubs, swarming bees and village interference, a pleasant and forgettable man in a good coat is making his patient way towards her. Romantic, funny and quietly lethal.

Themes

Love found late, hidden identity, second chances, courage, community, belonging, loyalty and the hive as a mirror for human devotion and danger.
