About the book

Dr Newman Stone keeps bees and keeps calm. In thirty-eight years, his hands have never once shaken. Then Cleo Marsh arrives in his Somerset village with no explanation anyone can draw from her, having chosen the one place where a person might disappear without realising it is also the one place where nothing stays hidden.

As honey shows, cooking clubs, village gossip and a matchmaking child pull Newman and Cleo together, a pleasant and forgettable man in a good coat is making his patient way towards her. The village sees a romance beginning. Cleo sees the past closing in. Newman sees neither, until the bees rise.

And when sixty thousand wings fill the air, stillness may be the only thing standing between Cleo and death.
