About the book

Be and Become is an illustrated personal development and educational workbook that turns everyday curiosity into real research. Through guided studies, field activities, observation and reflection, it helps young readers develop discipline, critical thinking, confidence and character.

From night skies and harbours to museums, wildlife and beehives, the book invites readers to observe, question, record and understand the world around them. Every observation becomes a lesson, every lesson becomes a step, and every step shapes the person they become.

A companion to Father’s Mistakes, Son’s Wisdom, and a complete standalone educational journey.

The first question is simple: what will you notice when you finally begin to look?
