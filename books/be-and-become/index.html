BeandBecome

an illustrated research workbook for young minds. Dr N T Pisharam

Eighteen real studies. Four days in the field. A notebook that turns a curious child into a working observer.

This is not a book of theory to read and forget. It is for you to do. Anyone can read; only the special ones can do.

Eighteen illustrated studies for readers aged ten and above, each a complete piece of real research, written straight onto the page: Introduction · Method · Results · Discussion.

Excellent for home schooling, independent learning, and holiday activities the whole family can enjoy together.

Garden Daya full day mapping, counting and sketching the life of one garden
Beekeeper Dayat the hives with a mentor, reading the ordered life of the colony
Night Sky Dayan evening looking up, keeping a sky log as astronomers do
Special Eventsthe unrepeatable moments, and how a prepared observer catches them

In the spirit of My Octopus Teacher
and the mentorship of The Karate Kid.

An Attenborough-warm family series in waiting: one child, one garden, one hive, one night sky.
