Some harms have no author. That is what makes them unstoppable.

A neurologist runs a deep-brain-stimulation trial from a quiet hospital on the edge of the Cumbrian fells. One by one, her patients rise and walk towards doors they should never open. There is nothing to find. No fault, no intruder, no crime on paper at all.

A harm with no fingerprints. The machine that leaves no author. Mercy and message hidden beneath the noise. Trust, belief and the consequences of invisible agency.

An ordinary hospital, sterile calm and rising dread. The only warning is a waveform slipping out of true and a patient walking into the dark.

A neurologist investigating unexplained behaviour in patients receiving deep-brain stimulation discovers a pattern designed to resemble chance. What begins on one ward threatens to spread across a country, and stopping it requires proving that something with no fingerprints exists at all.
