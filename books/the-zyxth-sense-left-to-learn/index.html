Jettisoned with a deadline: learn or never return.

Zyx Vorlan-7 fails the one exam his hyper-efficient planet never forgives, the affection assessment, and is thrown out of the sky over a Yorkshire common with four hidden hearts, a borrowed face, and four hundred days to learn what love actually is. He was told only to observe. He falls for a wedding planner instead.

Cast onto Earth as punishment, he loses everything that once made him powerful. Time is running out, and something hidden in plain sight may decide whether exile becomes extinction.

Learning to love without a manual. What a species-wide “affection assessment” can never measure. What is lost when you are ordered to observe a life instead of living it. How nine hundred years of policy loses to one very ordinary market town.

Big sky, bigger heart. An algorithm quietly losing its mind, a mediums' circle that is accidentally always right, and a visa clock that goes off at the worst possible moments.

For readers who want their science fiction gentle, their comedy warm, and their alien very well brought up.
