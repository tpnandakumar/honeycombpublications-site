Jettisoned with a deadline: learn or never return.

An alien with four hidden hearts has 400 days on Earth to understand love. He is told only to observe. He falls for a wedding planner instead.

Zyx Vorlan-7 fails the one assessment his hyper-efficient planet never forgives: affection. Cast down over Yorkshire with a borrowed human face and a ticking visa clock, he must learn what his civilisation cannot measure before the deadline sends him home forever.

Love beyond measurement. Observation versus participation. Exile, belonging, identity and the limits of systems that can quantify almost everything except what makes a life worth living.

A golden Yorkshire landscape, a four-hearted alien trying to behave impeccably, a wedding planner who keeps disrupting his instructions, and a visa clock that chooses the worst possible moments to remind him that time is running out.

Warm visual comedy, romance, speculative wonder and an escalating deadline.

Zyx Vorlan-7 comes from a civilisation built on efficiency, measurement and rules. When he fails its affection assessment, he is jettisoned to Earth with four hidden hearts, a borrowed face and 400 days to learn what love actually is.

His orders are simple: observe, learn and return. Yorkshire immediately makes that impossible. As friendships, village life and an increasingly important wedding planner pull him from detached observation into human attachment, the very experiences that may save him also make his mission harder to control.

With the visa clock running down and the affection protocol still judging him, Zyx must decide whether passing the test means obeying the system that exiled him, or becoming someone the system was never designed to understand.
