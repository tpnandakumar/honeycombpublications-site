# Honeycomb Publications Website Optimisation Study

## Study design

This is a **prospective longitudinal optimisation study**.

The study will follow the Honeycomb Publications website over time, collect repeated measurements, introduce controlled interventions and evaluate whether visibility, qualified visitors and conversion improve while abandonment falls.

## Three stages

1. **Visibility**: increase search impressions, indexing, referrals and discovery.
2. **Visitors**: convert visibility into qualified website sessions.
3. **Churn and conversion**: reduce abandonment and increase movement to retailer clicks and rights enquiries.

## Prospective features

- Outcomes are defined before data collection.
- Experiments are registered before deployment.
- Each page version is dated and reversible.
- Baseline and intervention periods are separated.
- Sample size, absolute change, relative change and uncertainty are reported.
- No invented or illustrative values may be presented as measured results.

## Longitudinal features

- Repeated weekly and cumulative measurements.
- Book-level, device-level and traffic-source trends.
- Monitoring for improvement, plateau, reversal and oscillation.
- Long-term retention of the strongest validated page configurations.

## Optimisation architecture

- The LLM generates and interprets controlled variants.
- The BBO engine ranks and selects candidates using real measured outcomes.
- PIMF may later analyse persistent higher-order change.

All changes must remain truthful, traceable, versioned and reversible. Sales may only be reported from retailer or distributor records.
