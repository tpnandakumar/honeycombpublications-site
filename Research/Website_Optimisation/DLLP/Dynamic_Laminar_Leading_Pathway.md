# Dynamic Laminar Leading Pathway

## Purpose

The Dynamic Laminar Leading Pathway, abbreviated **DLLP**, is the active routing mechanism that carries aligned information, computation and validated emergent capability through the Core–Stream–Conduit system towards the current position of maximum Efficiency Quotient.

The governing relation is:

> **DLLP → Max-EQ**

## Core principle

The system continuously creates, evaluates and refines the least wasteful validated route between the current problem state and the strongest attainable outcome.

The pathway must be:

- **Dynamic:** able to redirect as evidence, resources, objectives or conditions change.
- **Laminar:** coherent, low-friction and minimally turbulent.
- **Leading:** oriented towards the strongest validated outcome rather than passive historical repetition.
- **Efficient:** justified by total computational and operational cost.
- **Reversible:** versioned, evidence-linked and capable of rollback.

## Efficiency Quotient

The Efficiency Quotient is a comparative decision measure, not a claimed universal physical law.

`EQ = Validated Outcome Strength / Total System Cost`

Validated Outcome Strength may include:

- outcome quality;
- validation strength;
- generalisation;
- persistence;
- calibration;
- trust and factual integrity.

Total System Cost may include:

- evaluation count;
- runtime;
- memory;
- energy;
- data movement;
- switching overhead;
- coordination overhead;
- uncertainty;
- transfer risk;
- instability;
- implementation burden.

No EQ is calculated when required inputs are absent or denominators are zero.

## Max-EQ centre

The candidate with the highest validated EQ becomes the provisional centre only when:

- evidence quality is acceptable;
- minimum sample requirements are met;
- no critical linked outcome deteriorates materially;
- PIMF does not classify the result as transient noise, reversal or unresolved oscillation;
- human approval is recorded.

## Efficiency laminas

Candidates are arranged around the current Max-EQ centre.

### Central lamina

The current provisional Max-EQ candidate.

### Inner lamina

Near-optimal validated alternatives that provide resilience under changing constraints.

### Middle lamina

Conditional or specialist candidates that perform well only for particular books, Streams, audiences or resource conditions.

### Outer lamina

Exploratory, high-uncertainty, quarantined or not-yet-validated candidates.

The field recentres when a stronger validated candidate emerges.

## Laminar conduit flow

A Conduit is considered more laminar when it shows:

- clear direction;
- stable coupling;
- low duplicate computation;
- low contradictory routing;
- low switching frequency;
- preserved signal integrity;
- limited recirculation;
- acceptable latency;
- controlled cross-domain transfer.

## Turbulence indicators

- repeated Stream switching without gain;
- contradictory recommendations;
- oscillating objective weights;
- duplicated candidate evaluations;
- circular routing;
- unstable cross-domain transfer;
- abrupt cost increases;
- feedback amplification;
- rapid PIMF reversal or oscillation states;
- unexplained deterioration in linked outcomes.

## Laminarisation actions

The controller may recommend:

- narrowing or widening Conduit bandwidth;
- reducing or increasing coupling strength;
- damping update frequency;
- pausing an unstable Stream;
- changing Stream priority;
- imposing a computational budget;
- increasing evidence thresholds;
- opening a specialist bypass pathway;
- reverting to a validated inner-lamina candidate;
- quarantining an emergent behaviour.

These are recommendations until approved by a human operator.

## Emergent behaviour integration

The pathway integrates the A₃E-C₃MC framework:

- **A₃:** Aligned, Adaptive, Accommodative
- **E:** Efficient
- **C₃:** Coherent, Coordinated, Coupled
- **M:** Modulated
- **C:** Channelled

Raw emergence must be isolated, rationalised, modulated, channelled and validated before it can influence DLLP routing or Max-EQ centrality.

## Operating cycle

1. Read validated candidate and pathway observations.
2. Reject incomplete or invalid records.
3. Calculate outcome strength, system cost and EQ where possible.
4. Calculate turbulence and laminarity indicators.
5. identify the provisional Max-EQ candidate.
6. Assign candidates to efficiency laminas.
7. check PIMF state and linked-layer deterioration.
8. recommend retain, modulate, reroute, quarantine, promote or roll back.
9. await human approval for production action.
10. record the decision and Git commit.
