The author

Where the brain, the hive and the hidden world meet

The author is a neurologist and beekeeper, a spiritualist in outlook and a realist in judgement.

The author walks with reality, works with the brain, dances with the bees, and searches for the hidden mysteries of nature, science and the human spirit.

His writing grows from the meeting point of medicine, observation, philosophy and lived experience. It looks closely at the visible systems of the body and the world, while remaining alert to the deeper patterns, meanings and unanswered questions beneath them.

Across personal development, education, romantic comedy, fantasy and medical thrillers, the work returns to a common concern: how people learn, choose, endure, transform and become.
