# Honeycomb Publications Book Page Tab Standard

All major individual book pages use the same reader-facing tab sequence:

1. Logline
2. Premise
3. Themes
4. On Screen
5. Synopsis
6. Details
7. Where to Buy
8. Contact

## Rules

- **Logline:** 2 to 4 short lines maximum. It must communicate the dramatic or conceptual proposition immediately.
- **Premise:** the central setup and reason to enter the book.
- **Themes:** the major ideas explored by the work.
- **On Screen:** cinematic or factual-entertainment adaptation potential, visual world and dramatic treatment.
- **Synopsis:** a concise narrative or conceptual summary.
- **Details:** author, ISBNs, publication date, format, length and intended readership where applicable.
- **Where to Buy:** the shared international Amazon marketplace selector, with Kindle and Paperback routes kept separate from editorial content.
- **Contact:** screen, publishing, educational or institutional rights enquiries as relevant.

The navigation labels and order are fixed. Book-specific visual design and content remain individual to each title.
